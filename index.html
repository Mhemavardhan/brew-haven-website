Our Heritage

Where Passion for Coffee Meets Community Connection

Brew Haven began with a singular obsession: to honor the craft of coffee making from cherry to cup. What started as a micro-roastery has grown into a neighborhood sanctuary for students, professionals, and coffee purists alike.

We curate select single-origin coffees from Ethiopia, Colombia, Guatemala, and Coorg, roasting in small batches weekly to safeguard rich terroir, natural sweetness, and smooth finishes.

  • ✓ Sustainably sourced shade-grown whole beans
  • ✓ Custom calibrated water temperature and extraction
  • ✓ Dairy-free plant milk options: Oat, Almond, and Soy
  • ✓ Zero single-use plastic takeaway containers
